How we work

Five steps to a clean finish.

  1. Estimate

    Send wall sizes or photos. You get a fixed quote with no hidden costs.

  2. Visual & materials

    We show the layout on your room in 3D and bring finish samples to your home.

  3. Laser measure

    Every wall is measured on site so panels are cut to the millimetre.

  4. Installation

    Pre-cut panels go up in one to two days, with socket and TV cut-outs done in place.

  5. Finish & handover

    Filling, painting, a walkthrough together, and your written 10-year warranty.

Questions

Answered before you ask.

Will the work make a mess?

No. Panels are pre-cut in our workshop, we cover and protect everything, use dust extraction on site, and leave the room vacuumed and clean.

How long does a typical hallway take?

Most rooms are finished in one to two days. You'll get a clear timeline with your quote.

Do you design as well as install?

Yes. We can show the layout on your wall in 3D before we begin.

Is the quote really free?

Yes, with no obligation. Send wall sizes or photos and we'll come back with a fixed price.

Which areas do you cover?

All of Dublin, plus Kildare, Meath and Wicklow.

Is the work guaranteed?

Every installation comes with a written 10-year craftsmanship warranty. We carry full public and employer liability insurance.
